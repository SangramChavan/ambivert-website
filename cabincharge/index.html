How the concept works

From vehicle power to a wireless charging point.

The module is the charging transmitter. A complete cabin installation also needs a correctly rated, protected power-conversion stage and a mechanical enclosure designed for the cabin environment.

01

Vehicle electrical supply

Take power from an approved, protected vehicle circuit.

02

Regulated USB-C PD input

A suitable DC-DC / USB-C PD supply provides a supported input profile to the board.

03

Coil transfers energy

The control PCB drives the copper coil, which creates an alternating magnetic field.

04

Phone charges in place

A compatible receiver coil in the phone converts the field back into electrical power.

Important: do not wire the vehicle supply directly into the USB-C module. Use a protected automotive / industrial DC-DC converter with a compatible PD output.

Designed for integration

The module is the start.
The cabin system makes it work.

For heavy-equipment use, the mechanical and electrical integration is just as important as the charging board.

Mechanical design

Protect the coil and PCB behind a non-metallic charging surface. Design for phone retention, coil alignment, vibration, dust ingress and service access.

Holder · enclosure · mounting

Electrical & thermal design

Use a protected automotive/industrial DC-DC supply, appropriate fuse and wiring. Validate heat rise, ventilation, foreign-object heating and operation across cabin temperatures.
